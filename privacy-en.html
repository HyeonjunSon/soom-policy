Soom Privacy Policy

Draft (v1.0, 2026-07-23) — requires legal review before launch. This document is written to match the actual implementation; if the implementation changes, this document must change with it.

Soom (the "app") is an anonymous secret-exchange service. Not knowing who you are is the core of the product, and this policy spells out what that promise means.

1. What we collect

Account: you sign in with your Apple account or an email address (phone sign-in is not available yet). The only thing collected is your login identifier — and if you choose Apple's "Hide My Email," we only ever store the relay address Apple issues. We neither request nor collect your name, date of birth, location, contacts, or photos. There is no advertising SDK and no behavioral tracking.

The promise that matters: your login exists only to keep your account (alias, keys, secrets) yours. The only thing other users ever see is the alias you chose — secrets, comments, and 1:1 messages travel between people fully anonymously.

Data created while you use the app:

Item Contents Purpose
Account identifier Email address (or Apple relay address) + internal ID (UUID) Sign-in, keeping your balance and history
Alias The anonymous name you picked or rolled, and its orb colour The only thing shown to other users
Secrets you write Category, first line, body, chosen lifespan, timestamp Providing the exchange
Comments Free text you leave on a secret you opened (max 200 chars), timestamp The room of people who opened it
1:1 messages Messages exchanged with a secret's author (max 400 chars), read timestamps Providing the anonymous 1:1 chat
Hearts Which secrets you hearted Showing empathy, deciding permanent keeping
Unlock records Which secrets you spent a key on Free re-reads, room access
Key ledger Key earn/spend history (signup, purchase, unlock, daily, invite, share verification) Balance integrity, abuse prevention
Purchase records Key purchases (product ID, App Store transaction ID) Verifying/restoring purchases, abuse prevention
Invite records Your invite code, and who signed up with it Granting the invite reward
Share verifications Which platform you verified on, the capture image you uploaded, and when you may verify again Granting the reward, abuse review, cooldown
Reports & blocks What you reported, who you blocked Safety operations
Notifications Opened / reply / heart / expiry notices Showing your notifications
Age confirmation time Timestamp of your 17+ self-declaration Age-rating compliance

Your session token is stored only in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

2. The AI safety filter and third parties

Every secret, comment, and 1:1 message passes an automatic safety filter before it is posted or sent — regardless of any purchase. The filter runs in three stages, and in the process your text may be sent to the following processors:

Processor What is sent Purpose
Supabase Inc. All stored data listed above Database and server hosting
OpenAI (Moderation API) Only the text you attempted to post Scoring harm (sexual, hate, self-harm, threats)
Anthropic (Claude) Only the ambiguous minority of texts Context-aware final classification
Apple (App Store) Payment and receipt data for key purchases Processing payments, restoring purchases

Only anonymous text is sent to the filter. Your account identifier, alias, session token, and device information are never sent with it. Both OpenAI and Anthropic state in their policies that data sent through their APIs is not used to train their models (see each company's data-use policy).

Payments: key purchases are handled exclusively through the Apple App Store. Card numbers and other payment details never reach us; we store only the product identifier and receipt needed to verify and restore a purchase.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Secrets delete themselves. When the lifespan you chose (24 hours, 3 days, or 7 days) runs out, the body, the first line, the photo, and every comment attached to that secret are destroyed immediately. A hollow record remains only to label the room, and is deleted after 7 days if no conversation continues. A secret that passes 100 hearts is kept indefinitely (⭐); even then, its author can delete it at any time.
  • 1:1 messages outlive the secret. A conversation belongs to the two people in it, so it is kept separately from the secret's lifespan. Blocking the other person or deleting your account removes it, and account deletion below erases it all at any time.
  • Reported content is the one exception. When a report is filed, a copy of the reported secret, comment, or conversation is retained for up to one year — solely for handling the report, protecting users, and responding to legal requests — and is then destroyed. The copy is used for nothing else, even after the original has disappeared.
  • You can erase everything from inside the app: My space → Anonymity settings → "Delete account and data." This immediately and permanently deletes your account along with your secrets, comments, 1:1 messages, hearts, unlock records, key ledger, purchase records, share verifications, and reports/blocks. It cannot be undone.
  • Because the service is anonymous, we cannot locate a specific person's data by identity. Deletion is available only through the in-app function.

4. Age

Soom is for people 17 and older. The promises on the start screen include a 17+ self-declaration. If we learn that someone under 17 is using the app, we delete their data.

5. Self-harm and crisis

Text that indicates self-harm or crisis is not posted; instead we show crisis support resources for your region. This is not medical advice, and the app is not a substitute for emergency services.

6. Reporting and blocking

You can report a secret or a conversation from inside the app, and block anyone you are talking with. Blocking is mutual in effect: neither side sees the other's secrets or conversation again.

7. Your rights

Because the service is anonymous, we cannot process access or correction requests by identity. Instead, you can see all of your own data inside the app (your secrets, your conversations) and delete it at any time. Other questions: soom.teamcontact@gmail.com
