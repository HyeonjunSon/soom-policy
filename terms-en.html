  • The only thing other people see is the nickname you chose. There are no real names and no profile photos, by design.

3. Prohibited content and behaviour — zero tolerance

Soom has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. The following are not allowed anywhere — in secrets, comments or one-to-one chats.

  • Insults, threats, harassment or stalking aimed at a person
  • Hate speech (attacks based on race, origin, gender, sexual orientation, disability, religion and the like)
  • Sexual content, sexualisation, or sexual exploitation — and absolutely nothing involving minors
  • Planning, trading or encouraging illegal acts
  • Exposing someone's private information, impersonation, asking for contact details
  • Spam, advertising, repeated posting, automated accounts
  • Content that encourages self-harm or suicide, or describes how

What we do. Free text (secrets, comments, chats) must pass an automatic filter before it is posted. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; confirmed violations have their content removed and the author restricted or permanently banned. When reports from several different people pile up on the same post, it is hidden automatically before a human even looks. Serious violations are acted on immediately, without notice.

4. Reporting and blocking

  • Report: the ⋯ button on a secret, on a swipe card, on a comment, or in a chat — choose a reason and send.
  • Block: the same menu offers Block this person. Blocking hides your secrets, comments and chats from each other instantly, and the chat between you is deleted. Every block is also filed with our team for review.
  • Your blocked list lives in My space → Blocked, and you can undo a block at any time.
  • When something is reported, a copy of it is kept for up to one year for handling the report and for legal purposes (Privacy Policy, section 3).

5. What you post

  • What you write stays yours. You give us the permission we need to run the service — to store, show, deliver and safety-check it.
  • Secrets fade on their own. When the time you chose runs out (24 hours, 3 days, 7 days) the body, the first line, the photo and every comment on it are destroyed. A secret past 100 hearts stays as a ⭐. One-to-one chats outlive the secret they started from.
  • Faded content cannot be restored. Use the data export in My space if you want to keep a copy.

6. Keys and payments

  • You buy keys through the App Store, and you can also earn them by showing up daily, inviting friends, verified social sharing and rewarded ads. Keys obtained by abuse are revoked and the account may be restricted.
  • Refunds follow Apple's process (reportaproblem.apple.com).
  • If your account is deleted or banned for breaking these terms, remaining keys are forfeited.
